Smarteddance

Welcome To Smarteddance!

Smarteddance is a smart QR-based attendance management system that helps students mark attendance quickly and easily online.
Students can scan a QR code, select their course, and submit their attendance details digitally.

Qr Access

Student can easily access the attendance system by scanning a QR code using their mobile device

Course Selection

Student can select their registered course before submitting their attendance information

Digital Attendance

Attendance record are submitted electronically using student details such as name, matric number and department

Admin Dashboard

Lecturers and administrators can view and manage attendance records through a simple dashboard system.
